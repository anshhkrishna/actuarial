# Citation & Data Credits

This repository is analysis **code**. The data it analyzes is produced by the
U.S. National Center for Health Statistics (NCHS) and must be credited.

## Data sources

**NHANES (National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey), 1999–2000 and
2001–2002 cycles** — including the DNA Methylation Epigenetic Biomarker data.
Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), National Center for Health
Statistics (NCHS). Hyattsville, MD.
<https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/> · <https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/dnam/>

**NCHS Linked Mortality Files (public-use), 2019 linkage.** National Center
for Health Statistics. Mortality follow-up linked to the National Death Index
(NDI) through December 31, 2019.
<https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/data-linkage/mortality-public.htm>

Use of NHANES and the Linked Mortality Files is subject to the NCHS Data User
Agreement. Users must not attempt to re-identify participants.
